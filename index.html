01 / ABOUT THE SESSION

Connecting AI & Autism Research

First held in 2025 as part of the International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare (AIiH), the special session AI Innovations in Autism Diagnosis aims to bring together researchers and practitioners on an annual basis to explore the potential of AI in autism screening and diagnosis.

The session highlights advances in AI-driven approaches, including multimodal data analysis, machine learning and explainable AI, while considering the ethical and practical challenges of translating research into clinical practice.

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Early Detection and Screening
  • Eye-Tracking and Behavioural Data
  • Neuroimaging and Multimodal AI
  • Explainable and Trustworthy AI
  • LLMs and Generative AI
  • Clinical Translation and Ethics
